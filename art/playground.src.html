Crumb

A working preview of the desktop clean-up pet in 3D. Crumb is a chibi black puppy modeled in Blender, with jointed legs, ears, jaw, and tail, rendered on a transparent layer above your desktop icons. It faces you when it stops and turns toward wherever it walks. The junk files are the screenshots, installers, and duplicates.

    How the 3D Crumb works

    • Model: built in Blender by a script in the project (art/build_crumb.py) and loaded here as a 3D file. Every moving part hangs off a named joint.
    • Facing: it looks at you while idle, sitting, and asking. When it walks it turns three-quarters toward where it's going, and back to you when it stops.
    • Joints: four legs, head, jaw, two ears, and tail each rotate on their own pivot. The walk cycle is tied to distance so paws don't slide, ears flop on a spring, and the jaw opens for real bites.
    • Poses: stand, sit with a head tilt while asking, sniff, bite, carry a file in its mouth, curl up to sleep, and burp, all blending smoothly.
    • Placement: the 3D camera is lined up with screen pixels, so the puppy's nose lands exactly on the file it's biting or carrying.

    The Mac app loads the same Blender model (exported as USDZ) into RealityKit, Apple's current 3D engine, in a transparent window over the desktop, and moves the same joints in code.
